# Research design

## Objective

Estimate how adolescent socioeconomic risk is associated with STEM educational opportunity, educational attainment, employment access, and earnings by age 23 among Black girls and young Black women.

## Design

The primary design is an individual longitudinal cohort analysis using NLSY97. Respondents will contribute observations from ages 15 through 23. Age will be the primary time scale because respondents entered the survey at different ages and reached age 23 in different calendar years.

## Primary pathway

1. Adolescent socioeconomic risk
2. STEM educational opportunity
3. Educational attainment by age 23
4. Employment access at age 23
5. Labor earnings at age 23

## Planned methods

- Survey-weighted descriptive analysis
- Generalized structural equation modeling for direct and indirect associations
- Two-part earnings model for zero versus positive earnings
- Elastic-net models as a complementary predictive analysis
- Multiple imputation and complete-case sensitivity analysis
- Robustness checks for alternative construct definitions

## Interpretation boundary

The primary estimates are associational. Causal language will not be used unless the final identification strategy and assumptions support it.

## Data-access boundary

The initial implementation uses public-use NLSY97 files. Restricted geocode and school-survey files are outside the first-stage scope and must never be committed to the public repository.
