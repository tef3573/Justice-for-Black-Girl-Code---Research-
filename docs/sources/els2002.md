# Education Longitudinal Study of 2002 acquisition specification

## Role

ELS:2002 is a separate supplementary cohort for validating the operationalization
of STEM educational quality. It must not be merged person-to-person with NLSY97.

## Official public-use extraction scope

Request one combined Base Year through Third Follow-up public-use extract from
the NCES Online Codebook/EDAT, including prior waves. Select:

- respondent identifier and longitudinal weights;
- race/ethnicity and sex;
- family income, parent education, parent occupation, and official SES composites;
- school sector, urbanicity, resources, academic climate, and course offerings;
- high-school mathematics/science coursetaking composites available publicly;
- mathematics assessment scores;
- high-school completion and postsecondary enrollment/attainment;
- employment status/history, work hours, earnings, and perceived employment barriers;
- response-status and imputation flags.

## Access boundary

The detailed ELS:2002 high-school transcript component is restricted-use, although
some public-use transcript/coursetaking composites are available. Only public-use
fields may enter this repository. Restricted school or transcript identifiers and
microdata require separate licensing and secure storage.

## Acquisition status

Registered and specified; public-use extract generation remains pending in the
official NCES interface. No placeholder or third-party copy will be substituted.
